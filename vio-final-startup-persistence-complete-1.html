Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Ruucho is a social platform built for creators. We believe good work deserves to be found, no matter how many followers you have.

We bring together social networking, creator discovery, and a built-in economy so every creator has a real shot at building an audience.

Our discovery engine looks at the quality of your work, not your follower count. A creator with 50 followers can reach the same audience as one with 50,000 if their content is valuable.

Your visibility score in Creator Analytics shows how the engine is surfacing your work. Create consistently and engage authentically. The engine handles the rest.

Vicoins are the currency of the Ruucho economy. You earn them by creating content, engaging with the community, and growing on the platform.

Your balance lives in your Wallet. Soon you will be able to spend Vicoins in the Marketplace to offer services, buy exclusive content, and support other creators.

Yes. You control your privacy and safety on Ruucho. You can make your account private, choose who can comment and tag you, block accounts, mute words, and filter sensitive content.

Find all of these controls in Settings, then Privacy & Safety.

Verified creators get better discovery and trust signals on Ruucho. You can apply through the side drawer, then Verification.

We grant verification based on the authenticity and quality of your work, not your follower count. We review each application manually to keep things fair.

Tap Report a Problem below to open the support form. Describe the issue, pick a category, and submit. Your report is saved locally and will reach our support team once the backend is connected.

For urgent safety concerns, choose the Safety Concern category and include as much detail as you can.
